# COMPETITOR ANALYSIS — Top 5 & how we beat them
(Research: Aug 2026 web sources)

| # | Competitor | Kya achha karte hain | Gap jo hum bharte hain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **NoimosAI** — "personal AI marketing team 24/7", finished work approval feed | Closest positioning; approval feed; multi-channel | Feed = list. Hamara approval EXPERIENCE hai (live office, named agents, pipeline you can watch). + tokens transparent, unka pricing opaque |
| 2 | **Sight AI** — 13+ content agents, Autopilot, AI-visibility (GEO) tracking | GEO angle smart; auto-indexing | Content-only (no leads/reviews/site-care). Hum GEO ko roadmap pe rakhte hain lekin bundle jeet-ta hai |
| 3 | **HubSpot Breeze** | Deep CRM integration, trust | Enterprise pricing/complexity; SMB ke liye overkill. Hum: $0 start, 2-min setup |
| 4 | **Copy.ai (GTM AI)** | Strong workflows, B2B GTM | Operator-driven (tum chalao); no visible team, no approval-first identity |
| 5 | **Jasper** | Brand voice, content pipelines | Tool, not team: no autonomy, no leads, no daily reports; $39+/mo |

## Landing/SEO patterns unse liye (aur behtar kiye)
- Comparison table on landing (sab karte hain) → humne "you can SEE the team" row add ki jo koi match nahi kar sakta
- FAQ + schema.org JSON-LD (FAQPage + SoftwareApplication) — rich results ke liye
- Outcome-led headings, not feature-led
- Unki weakness: sab ke dashboards faceless — hamara office section landing pe hi LIVE demo hai (show, don't tell)

## SEO roadmap (post-launch)
Use-case landing pages per vertical (clinics/agencies/local services), product blog dogfooded by the product itself, GEO: track brand mentions in ChatGPT/Claude answers (Sight AI ka pattern) — Phase 3.
